Edit your portfolio

Your content is edited in Pages CMS. Changes are saved to GitHub and the live site updates within a few minutes.

  1. Open the editor and sign in with GitHub.
  2. Choose your portfolio repository, then the section you want: Projects, Experience, Site settings and so on.
  3. Edit, upload screenshots or CVs, then click Save.
  4. Wait two to three minutes and refresh your site.
